Privacy Policy — VideoCraft

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Contact: videocrafteditor@gmail.com

VideoCraft ("we", "us", "the app") is a video editing application for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles, who receives it, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • VideoCraft edits your videos on your device. Your photos, videos, audio and projects are not uploaded to us.
  • There is no account system.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may receive device identifiers and related data to show those ads.
  • If you buy VideoCraft Pro, Google Play processes the payment and RevenueCat helps us confirm your purchase. We never see your payment card details.
  • We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes.
  • We do not sell your data for money. Some advertising data sharing may count as a "sale" or "sharing" under certain laws. See section 10.

1. Photos, videos and audio you choose

When you add media to a project, the app reads the files you select and stores its working copies on your own device. Editing, effects and export are performed locally on your phone.

  • We do not upload your media to any server.
  • Exported videos and images are saved to your device's gallery (Movies/VideoCraft, Pictures/VideoCraft).
  • If you choose to share an export to another app, such as WhatsApp, that app's own privacy policy applies to what you send.
  • Deleting a project or uninstalling the app removes the app's local copies.

Photo and video library access

To let you browse and pick your media inside the app, VideoCraft asks for permission to read your photo and video library. This is used only to show you a gallery of your own media and to import the items you choose.

  • The gallery is displayed on your device. We do not upload, index, scan or transmit your library.
  • Only the items you select are copied into your project.
  • The app does not request access to the location data stored inside photos.
  • On older Android versions, the app uses storage permission only to read the media you pick and to save your exports to your gallery.
  • On Android 14 and later you may grant access to selected items only.
  • If you decline, you can still import media through your device's own file picker.

You can change or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

2. Microphone

If you use the Voiceover feature, the app requests microphone permission and records audio. The recording is saved locally into your project and is not transmitted to us. You may decline, and the rest of the app continues to work.

3. Notifications and background work

The app may ask to show notifications so you can see export progress and cancel an export. It may keep working in the background while an export finishes. This data stays on your device.

4. Internet use

The app connects to the internet to:

  • download optional content you request, such as templates and music, from our content storage, which is hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare receives the request, including your IP address;
  • download a font from Google Fonts if you choose one that is not included in the app. Google receives the request, including your IP address, as described at https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy;
  • request and display ads in the free version (section 6);
  • confirm purchases (section 7);
  • send crash diagnostics (section 5).

5. Crash reporting and diagnostics

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to learn about crashes and errors so we can fix them. A report may include:

  • the error and stack trace,
  • device model, operating-system version and app version,
  • a randomly generated installation identifier, and
  • how long the app ran before the crash.

We use this data only to diagnose stability problems. We do not intend it to include your media or projects, although error text may occasionally contain technical details such as a file name. Firebase's handling of this data is described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Advertising

The free version of the app displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped,
  • diagnostic information about ad performance, and
  • device and app information.

Google's practices are described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • In regions where consent is required, including the EU, EEA and UK, and where US state privacy rules apply, the app shows a consent form from Google before ads are personalised.
  • Where this applies, you can review or change that choice from the "Privacy options" item on the app's credits screen.
  • You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings, usually under Privacy and then Ads.
  • If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads, for which limited data is still used for purposes such as measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Users with VideoCraft Pro do not see ads.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play. Google's privacy policy applies to your payment. We never receive or store your payment card or banking details.

We use RevenueCat to manage and confirm your Pro status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription information from Google Play, and device and technical data such as app version and IP address. This data is used to unlock paid features and to restore purchases. RevenueCat's policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

We see purchase records, such as product, status and dates, linked to that anonymous identifier. We do not receive your name or email address from RevenueCat unless you send them to us yourself.

8. Reviews

The app may ask for a rating using Google Play's in-app review feature. Google handles that review under its own terms.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are below the age of digital consent in your country, ask a parent or guardian before using the app. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, write to videocrafteditor@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Your rights

VideoCraft is available worldwide. The app has no accounts, and your media stays on your device. The personal data involved is the crash, advertising and purchase data described above.

EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR and UK GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. Our legal bases are: legitimate interest for crash diagnostics; your consent for personalised advertising, which you can withdraw at any time as described in section 6; legitimate interest for non-personalised advertising; and performance of a contract for purchases. The data controller is the VideoCraft developer, contactable at the address below.

California and other US states with privacy laws. You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, including for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. However, the advertising data described in section 6 may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" under these laws. You can opt out using the "Privacy options" item described in section 6. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.

India. Under applicable Indian data protection law, you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to raise a grievance, by writing to the address below.

Everywhere else. We will honour access and deletion requests on the same terms.

To exercise these rights, write to videocrafteditor@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. Because the app has no accounts, we may need information to find your records, such as your Google Play order number for purchases. Crash and advertising data held by Google may not be linkable to you by us. You can also use the controls described above.

11. Data retention

Your content stays on your device until you delete a project or uninstall the app. Crash diagnostics, advertising data and purchase records are retained by Google and RevenueCat according to their published policies, and for as long as needed for purchases and legal obligations.

12. International data transfers

Crash, advertising and purchase data may be processed by Google, its partners and RevenueCat on servers outside your country, including in the United States. These companies describe the safeguards they use in the policies linked above. Your photos, videos and projects are not transferred by us.

13. Security

Data sent by the app to these services is encrypted in transit.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised policy at the same address.

15. Contact

Questions about privacy: videocrafteditor@gmail.com
